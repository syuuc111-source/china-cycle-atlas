DATA ATTRIBUTION AND LICENSES

1. Chinese asterism lines and traditional star names
Source: Stellarium, skycultures/chinese/index.json and description.md
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/chinese
Contributors: Karrie Berglund, Sun Shuwei, Stellarium contributors.
Based on Yi Shitong, Chinese and Western Contrast Star Chart and Catalogue 1950.0;
Complete Studies of Astronomical (Yi Xiang Kao Cheng) and later revisions.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: selected the 28 main namesake asterisms; retained HIP-based lines;
converted the JSON structure; selected Chinese names for included stars.
The adapted asterism and name dataset in data/sky.json is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
No constellation artwork or reference video frames are included.

2. Stellar coordinates and magnitudes
Source: D3-Celestial, stars.8.json (J2000 equatorial directions).
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial
Original astronomical source credited by D3-Celestial:
XHIP: An Extended Hipparcos Compilation, Anderson E., Francis C. (2012), VizieR V/137D.
Changes: selected stars with magnitude <= 5.4 plus all asterism and focus members;
packed HIP, RA in degrees, declination in degrees, and apparent magnitude into arrays.
